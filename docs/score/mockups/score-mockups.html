Score editor mockups

These are the screens for the notation editor described in the Score editor plan. The first one is a working prototype: you can enter notes, select and copy bars, swap instruments, drag a dynamics segment, loop a passage and play it. The others are layouts drawn from the same example score.

Screen 1 · working prototype

Score view

The main window. Each line is a staff with its instrument, mute, solo, duplicate and volume on the left. The time ruler on top is the scrub and loop track. Violin I has its dynamics lane open: the dashed curve is what the printed marks produce, and the blue segment in bar 2 is an override.

Enter notes. Press N (or Note input), then click a line or space. The ghost note is already spelled for the key: the top line in G major is F♯. Pick a duration first with the palette or Alt+1–6.
Build chords. With a note selected, 2–9 add that interval above in the key, Shift below. + - 0 make it sharp, flat or natural; ↑/↓ move it a semitone, Alt a step, Ctrl an octave.
Voices. Choose voice 2 and click into a staff: it gets its own stems-down line. The piano's right hand already has two.
Bars. Click empty space in a bar to select it; Ctrl+click takes it in every line, Shift+click extends. Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy and paste, R repeats the selection after itself (the score grows).
Play. Space plays from the green playhead. Click a note or the ruler to move it. Drag along the ruler to set a loop, or select bars and press L. The tempo slider changes playback only.
Lines. Click an instrument name to swap it, even while playing; the notes stay. The two-squares button duplicates a line. Drag the blue dynamics segment up or down and hear Violin I change in bar 2.
Screen 2 · drawn from the same score

Timeline view

The DAW side of the same file. Each line is a track; its notes show as a strip, and automation lanes open under it. The bottom pane is a piano roll of the selected line. Edits in the score view appear here, and this view's playhead moves with playback.

Screen 3 · faders are live

Mixer

One strip per line with its insert effects, two sends and pan, then the reverb and delay buses and the master chain. The faders control the same volume as the score's line headers, and the meters move during playback.

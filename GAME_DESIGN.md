This has already been built, no reason to re-describe everything here

# Level 2

The player exits a small canyon to come across a sprawling tiered enemy fortification (I'm thinking the kind of ridiculously large scale that Warhammer 40k has) with a dramatic battle happening, dozens of enemy defenders fighting dozens of allies who are rushing the fortifications in an effort to capture them. Explosions all around, star fighters flying overhead chasing each other, parts of the fortifications being destroyed by tanks, a super dramatic set piece. The player has to fight up the tiers and through the fortifications (this level should be roughly 3x the length of level1) until they get to the top of the hill at which point they clear out all the enemies. Once all the enemies are dead, an animation plays of some of their allies lowering the enemy flag on the top of the hill and raising theirs. The level should be relatively linear in terms of leading the player along, we want to be able to have them play with no waypoints system

# Level 3

The player starts out riding in a troop transport through a city at night with rain falling. The convoy slowly moves through urban streets, stopping in the city center square where everyone unloads. Suddenly enemies appear in the streets and windows of buildings, ambushing the player and allies. The player and allies must push into the large building at the top of the square, fight through it to clear out the building, and then exit out the back moving through narrow streets fighting enemies. I want dramatic set pieces where the enemy collapse buildings to crush allied vehicles and try to stop the progression of the player and I want super tense close quarters tight urban combat. The player and allies will have to push through the city until they reach a sports arena, at which point they will have to fight to the center where they can then capture a "command" point. After capturing this, allied dropships should land and disembark reinforcements. The player should then have to fight through the city to three other command points, capturing each of them as they go with a sort of open-world-ish battleground in the sense of they can choose the order they follow to capture the points. As the player captures command points they should get reinforcements and the remaining ones should be come more and more defended. Once capturing all of them, the player should have to push to the outside of the city to drive out the last of the enemy. After doing that I want allied dropships to land a reinforcements and the level ends. As part of this, add a compass feature in the top of the UI and each command point should have an objective marker in the compass
